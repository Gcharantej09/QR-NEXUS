About QR Nexus

QR Nexus is a smart traffic document management system designed to make vehicle document verification faster, paperless, and trustworthy — for every citizen of India.

Citizens can store their driving license, RC, and pollution certificate details in a secure QR code. Traffic officers can instantly scan and verify these documents on the spot — with no paperwork, no queues, and no delays.

Built with Firebase for secure, real-time data management and 256-bit AES encryption for citizen privacy.
